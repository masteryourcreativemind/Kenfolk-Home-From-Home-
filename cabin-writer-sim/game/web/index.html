    A WORLD YOU CAN EXPLORE

    Pinewood map

    Gold marks you. Colored dots mark villagers; pale dots mark animals. Click a building door to walk there. Journeys cost game time, not credits.

    This time, you are the player.

    Move with WASD or the arrow keys, the on-screen pad, or by clicking ground. Step through marked doors to enter buildings and through the floor exit to leave.

    E interacts, M opens your map, B opens your body, I opens your bag, and J opens your journal. Stand within two tiles of a villager or workstation to use it.

    Start with Nessa's fishing quest at the lake. Your rod, bow, raincoat, meals and supplies are already packed. Hunting needs an animal within six tiles and an unobstructed shot; wildlife roams and replenishes daily.

    Eat, drink, rest and wash. Cook raw catches at a lit stove. Rain wets you, uplands are colder, and thick clothes can overheat you. Check individual organs, body temperature, water and blood, not only total HP.

    Talk to Mira to trade, Jun for treatment, Rowan for a hunting quest and Ada for writing advice. You can still research and write books that earn credits and download as EPUB/PDF bundles.

    Time passes only through your actions. Every action saves automatically. Download a backup before choosing New life. On restart, your saved player life resumes automatically.
